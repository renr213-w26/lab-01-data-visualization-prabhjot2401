p<-ggplot(midwest,aes(x=percollege,y=percbelowpoverty,colour = state,shape = state))+geom_point()+labs(x="Percentage with College Degree",y="Percentage Below Poverty",title="Percentage of People with College Degree vs Below Poverty",subtitle = "By State")
p
ggplotly(p)

Comment

There is a downward trend between the percentage of people with a college degree and the percentage of people below the poverty line, which means that, generally, as people become more educated, they rise above the poverty line. There is one noticeable outlier: Menominee County in Wisconsin, where 7.3% of the population has a college degree and 48.7% are below the poverty line. In most counties, the general trend of decay holds, with some dissimilarities, such as in Ohio, where counties fall within a similar range, and in Illinois, which has a significant outlier: Jackson County, where 36.6% of people have a college degree and 28.4% are below the poverty line.
